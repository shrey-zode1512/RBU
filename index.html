Electronics Product Sales Data Analysis

A data science project that cleans a US electronics sales dataset, analyses it with Pandas and shows the results with Matplotlib and Seaborn charts.

View dataset on Kaggle See the workflow

The dataset

Each row is one product in one order, from an electronics store in 9 US cities (April to September 2019).

Work division

The project was split into three parts.

Loading and cleaning

Opens the file, checks missing values and duplicates, and fixes data types and dates.

Pandas operations

Creates the Revenue column, filters, groups, ranks products and builds summary tables.

Matplotlib and Seaborn

Draws the charts and writes what each chart shows.
